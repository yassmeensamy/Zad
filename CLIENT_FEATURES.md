# Zaad (زاد) — Features Overview

**Zaad turns learning the Islamic sciences into a daily habit the whole family looks forward to — through bite-sized quizzes, friendly competition, streaks, and team play, online or offline, in Arabic and English.**

Zaad is a polished, modern mobile app that makes Islamic learning engaging and rewarding. It combines a smooth quiz experience, family-friendly multi-profile accounts, social team competition, and a fully offline-capable design — all wrapped in a clean, elegant interface that adapts to light and dark mode.

---

## 1. Sign In & Account

A flexible, welcoming entry experience that lets anyone start instantly and commit later.

- **Email & password sign-up** with a secure email verification code (OTP).
- **Log in** with email and password.
- **Sign in with Google** — one tap, no typing.
- **Try it as a guest** — start learning immediately, no account required.
- **Upgrade from guest to a full account at any time — without losing any progress.**
- **Forgot your password?** Reset it securely with a verification code.
- **Log out** and **switch between accounts** (for example, from a parent to a child profile).

---

## 2. Profiles & Family Accounts

Built for individuals and for whole families under one roof.

- Choose your account type: **Individual** or **Family**.
- Complete your profile with name, birthday, gender, and a personal **avatar**.
- **Family mode:** parents can create and manage **multiple children**, each with their own avatar and (optionally) their own password.
- Switch easily into any child's profile.
- Edit your profile and change your avatar anytime.
- Reset your learning progress for a fresh start.

---

## 3. Learning & Quizzes — The Heart of the App

A focused, satisfying learning loop designed to keep users coming back.

- Browse **categories** and the **levels** inside each one (clearly marked as locked, in progress, or completed).
- Play a quiz by answering questions **one at a time**, each with four answer choices.
- Get **instant feedback** on every answer, including a clear **explanation and source**.
- **Smart retry:** any question you get wrong comes back around until you answer it correctly — so learning actually sticks.
- **Earn points** as you go: the faster you answer correctly, the more points you earn.
- **Celebrations** appear when you do well — rewarding moments that make progress feel great.
- **Review mode:** revisit any completed level with all the correct answers revealed.
- **Save a question** to your personal collection with your own note.
- **Report a question** if something looks off — it goes straight to support.
- Move **back and forth** through the questions you've already answered.

---

## 4. My Drafts (Personal Saved Questions)

A private notebook of the questions that matter to each user.

- Keep a personal list of **saved questions with your own notes**.
- **Create, edit, and delete** saved items freely.
- Works **fully offline** — anything you save syncs automatically once you're back online.

---

## 5. Teams & Friendly Competition

Turn solo study into a social, motivating experience.

- **Create your own team** — you become the team owner.
- **Join a team with a simple invite code**, a shared link, or a QR code.
- **Invite others** by sharing a link or QR code.
- See your **team's leaderboard**, your team's **global rank**, and a **live activity feed** of what teammates are doing.
- **Leave a team** anytime.
- **Transfer ownership** to another member when you want to hand over the team.

---

## 6. Progress, Streaks & Leaderboards

Visible momentum that keeps motivation high.

- **Leaderboard:** see global rankings, switch between **Individuals** and **Teams**, filter by category, and find **your own rank** instantly.
- **Streaks:** track your **current and longest streak**, with a **7-day activity grid** that shows your consistency at a glance.
- **Quiz stats:** weekly questions solved, a **day-by-day breakdown**, your total solved, and your personal rank.
- A **daily Quran sign** card greets users on the home screen.

---

## 7. Works Offline — Anywhere, Anytime

Learning shouldn't stop when the connection does.

- **Download categories** to your device for **offline play**.
- **Take quizzes and save notes with no internet at all** — everything you do is stored safely and **synced automatically** the moment you reconnect.
- A clear indicator keeps users aware of their online/offline status.

---

## 8. Notifications

Gentle, controllable nudges that bring users back.

- A built-in **notification inbox** — read, delete, and mark all as read.
- **Push notifications** keep users informed and engaged.
- Full control: users can **turn each notification type on or off** in their preferences.

---

## 9. Deep Linking — One-Tap Team Joins

Frictionless sharing that brings new members straight into the action.

- **Tap a shared team invite link and the app opens directly on the Join Team screen, with the invite code already filled in.**
- Works as a proper **Universal Link (iOS) / App Link (Android)** — the link opens the app, not a browser.
- Smart and reliable: the link respects sign-in state, so users are guided to sign in first if needed, then continue straight to joining the team.

---

## 10. Help & Support

Users are never left stuck.

- **Help Center:** choose a topic and send a message in seconds.
- **Support tickets:** view your conversations, **reply** in a threaded view, and **close** tickets when resolved.

---

## 11. Personalization

The app adapts to each user's preferences.

- **Two languages:** Arabic and English, switchable anytime.
- **Light, Dark, or System theme** — beautifully designed for both, with a warm, elegant palette.
- **Automatic app updates** prompt users to stay on the latest version.

---

## In Short

Zaad delivers a **complete, polished learning ecosystem**: instant onboarding for anyone, real depth for committed learners, family accounts, social team competition, streaks and leaderboards for motivation, and a true offline-first design — all in a refined, bilingual, light-and-dark interface that feels great to use every single day.
